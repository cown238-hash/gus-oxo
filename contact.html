FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our services

How long does it take to complete a project?

Project timelines vary depending on complexity. A typical beat takes 3-5 days, while mixing & mastering takes 1-3 days. We'll provide a specific timeline during consultation.

Do you offer revisions?

Yes! We offer 2 free revisions for all services. Additional revisions can be arranged for a small fee.

What file formats do you deliver?

We deliver high-quality WAV and MP3 files for audio, and MP4 for video projects. Stems are available upon request for mixing & mastering services.

Can I get exclusive rights to a beat?

Yes! Exclusive rights are available for all beats. Contact us for pricing and details on exclusive licenses.
